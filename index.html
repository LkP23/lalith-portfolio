About Me

I am just beginning my Ph.D. journey in Management at Wayland Baptist University. As I start this path, I am developing research interests around the intersection of computer networking, data analytics, and business decision-making.

I am especially curious about how network performance, API ecosystems, and data flows affect real business outcomes like service quality, risk, compliance, and operational efficiency.

Over time, I hope to build a research direction that connects technical system behavior with practical business value in enterprise and digital environments.

Research Interests

Right now, I am interested in exploring how network-level observability can support better business decisions. I want to study how network performance, APIs, and data movement influence outcomes such as revenue, risk, compliance, and service reliability. Since I am at the beginning of my Ph.D., these interests are still taking shape, but they reflect the direction I want to grow into through research.

Research Directions

These are some of the areas I hope to explore during my Ph.D. They are not finished projects, but early research directions that I want to learn more about and develop further.

Network Latency and Business Impact

One area I want to explore is how API latency and network performance relate to business outcomes such as user experience, SLA compliance, and operational efficiency.

I am interested in understanding whether technical performance signals can be connected in a meaningful way to business-facing metrics and decision-making.

Areas I want to explore: Time-series analysis, performance monitoring, KPI modeling

Data Flow Traceability and Governance

Another area I want to study is how data moves across APIs and distributed systems, and how that movement can be made more transparent, traceable, and easier to govern.

I see this as a possible path toward research in compliance, data lineage, and monitoring frameworks for enterprise systems.

Areas I want to explore: Data lineage, API monitoring, governance, compliance
