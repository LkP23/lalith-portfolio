About Me

I am a Ph.D. Scholar in Management with research interests at the intersection of computer networking, management, data analytics, and enterprise decision systems. My work focuses on transforming network-level observability into business decision intelligence by studying how network performance, API ecosystems, and data flows influence outcomes such as revenue, risk, compliance, and service quality.

I am particularly interested in designing network-aware decision systems that integrate observability, governance, and economic optimization across enterprise platforms.

Research Directions

The following research directions represent my planned work during my Ph.D. These areas focus on integrating network-level observability with business decision systems.

Planned Research: Network Latency Impact on Business KPIs

This research aims to study how API latency and network performance influence business outcomes such as revenue, user engagement, and SLA compliance.

The goal is to develop models that connect network-level metrics with business decision indicators.

Planned Methods: Time-series analysis, causal inference, KPI modeling

Planned Research: Data Flow Traceability and Governance Systems

This research focuses on designing frameworks to track data movement across APIs and distributed systems for improved governance and compliance.

The objective is to build auditable and transparent systems for enterprise data flow monitoring.

Focus Areas: Data lineage, API monitoring, compliance systems
